Data-subject rights

KVKK Request Procedure

Use the steps below to securely submit an information, access, correction, deletion, or objection request concerning your personal data.

Electronic request channel: Submit your request securely from the email address registered to your account.

Available requests

  • Learn whether personal data is processed and request information if it is
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used accordingly
  • Learn which third parties receive the data
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data
  • Request deletion or destruction where legal conditions are met
  • Request notice of corrections or deletions to data recipients
  • Object to an adverse outcome based solely on automated processing
  • Request compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing

How to submit a request

Send an email from your registered account address to kvkk@tahosapp.com.tr with the subject “KVKK Data Subject Request.” Include:

  1. Your first name, last name, and tahosapp username
  2. The email address registered to your account
  3. A clear, specific, and understandable description of the request
  4. Relevant transaction date, server, or message information, if available
  5. Your preferred response method

Secure verification

To ensure a request does not target another account, we may send a verification code to the registered email address or ask for additional information. Never send your password, one-time login code, full identity-document image, or unnecessary sensitive data by email.

Response time and fees

Requests are handled as soon as possible according to their nature and generally no later than 30 days. If processing creates an additional cost, a fee under the tariff set by Turkey's Personal Data Protection Board may apply.

Refusal or additional information

A request may be partially or fully refused if it affects another person's rights, identity cannot be verified, the request is unclear, or retention is legally required. The reason will be explained in the response.
