Under Turkey's Law No. 6698

Privacy and KVKK Notice

This notice explains which personal data is processed when you use tahosapp, why it is processed, who may receive it, and your rights.

Effective: September 8, 2026Version: 1.1Contact: kvkk@tahosapp.com.tr
Privacy contact: Send requests to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your personal data to kvkk@tahosapp.com.tr.

1. Scope

This notice covers personal-data processing on tahosapp.com.tr, the tahosapp web and Windows applications, the API, real-time communication features, and related support processes.

2. Categories of processed data

CategoryExamples
Identity and accountUsername, email address, user ID, display name, avatar, biography, and preferences.
AuthenticationArgon2id password hash, email-verification status, one-time verification records, session details, and tokens. Plain-text passwords are never stored.
Communications and contentMessages, direct and group messages, attachments, reactions, polls, reports, and support correspondence.
CommunitiesServer membership, roles, permissions, invitations, channel actions, moderation decisions, blocks, and audit logs.
Technical and securityIP address, timestamps, request and error logs, client or device type, app version, and abuse indicators.
Voice and videoVoice-room membership and microphone, camera, or stream status. Audio, camera, and screen streams are not recorded by the server during normal use; they are transmitted through WebRTC or TURN where necessary.
Activity statusIf enabled by the user: games, applications, track or media names, playback status, and Rich Presence preferences.
Spotify invitationsSpotify track URL, title, artwork, and optional playback position. OAuth tokens are stored per user in encrypted application state when account integration is available.
FeedbackAggregated feedback such as uninstall reason and app version; it is not linked to an account unless free text identifies one.

3. Processing purposes

  • Create accounts, verify email, authenticate users, and protect accounts
  • Provide messaging, files, friends, servers, channels, voice, video, streaming, and music invitations
  • Apply roles and permissions, support moderation, and protect community safety
  • Remember notification, preference, device, and accessibility settings
  • Troubleshoot errors, measure performance, and detect attacks or abuse
  • Meet legal obligations and handle rights requests and disputes

4. Collection methods and legal grounds

Data is collected through the website, Windows application, API, real-time connections, user actions, and security logs, automatically or directly from users. Processing may rely on forming or performing a contract, legal obligations of the data controller, establishing, exercising, or protecting a right, and legitimate interests that do not override fundamental rights. Where law requires consent for a separate activity, consent will be requested freely and separately.

5. Recipients, service providers, and international transfers

Data may be shared only as necessary and for stated purposes with hosting and network providers, Cloudflare security and CDN services, email delivery providers, TURN and PeerJS communication infrastructure, GIPHY when a user searches for GIFs, and Spotify when a user shares, previews, opens, or connects a Spotify track. Data is disclosed to public authorities only where legally required. If provider servers or support teams are outside Turkey, international-transfer rules may apply; optional transfers should not occur without the required legal mechanism.

6. Retention

Account and content data is kept while the service continues or the account remains active; security logs are limited to threat investigation and dispute periods; legally required records are kept for the applicable statutory period. When the purpose ends, data is deleted, destroyed, or anonymized. Deletion from backups may complete during the secure backup cycle.

7. Security measures

Controls include Argon2id password hashing, optional AES-256-GCM encrypted application storage, TLS, access controls, role and permission validation, rate limiting, file type and size checks, and event logs. See the security page for details.

8. Your rights under KVKK

Under Article 11 of Turkey's KVKK, you may ask whether your personal data is processed, request information, learn its purpose and whether it is used accordingly, learn the recipients, request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, request deletion or destruction where conditions are met, request notice of correction or deletion to recipients, object to adverse automated-analysis outcomes, and seek compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. Use the KVKK Request page.

9. Children's privacy

The service is not intended for anyone under 13. Minors may use it only under parent or legal-guardian supervision and in accordance with applicable law. If we learn of an account belonging to someone under 13, review and deletion may begin.

10. User controls

Notification, device, voice, Rich Presence, and activity visibility settings are managed in the app. Local data can be cleared through browser or application settings. Account access, correction, and deletion requests may be sent through support or the KVKK channel.

11. Changes

This notice may be updated when the service or law changes. Material changes will be announced through an appropriate in-app notice, and the effective date appears above.
