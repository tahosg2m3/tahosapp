Product features

Everything you need to talk, share, and build communities.

tahosapp brings messaging, voice, video, streaming, events, and permission tools together in an independent platform for everyday conversations and large communities.

Every current feature is free: 4K streaming, GIF avatars, ten app themes, eight profile themes, name fonts and effects, avatar decorations, custom emoji and stickers, and advanced server profiles are available without a paywall.

Real-time messaging

Text channels, direct messages, and group conversations update instantly across devices through Socket.IO live events.

  • Send, edit, delete, reply to, and react to messages
  • Threads, pinned messages, unread indicators, and typing status
  • Images, files, GIFs, emoji, polls, voice messages, and Spotify track invitations
  • Message search, notification preferences, and user blocking
  • Friend requests, one-to-one chats, and group direct messages

Voice chat and clear communication

WebRTC voice channels are designed for low-latency communication. Optional RNNoise suppression helps reduce keyboard, fan, and background noise.

  • Mute, deafen, and push to talk
  • Input and output device selection, sensitivity, and microphone testing
  • Echo cancellation, automatic gain, and standard or strong noise suppression
  • Per-user volume and a speaking indicator
  • Join, leave, move, and other feedback sounds
  • Moderator controls for moving, muting, deafening, and disconnecting members

Camera, video calls, and screen sharing

Camera and screen streams can be viewed inside a voice room, expanded, and opened full screen.

  • Camera controls and device selection
  • Screen or application-window sharing
  • Room, grid, expanded, and full-screen viewing
  • Streaming controls and viewer feedback

Direct calls

Friends can start one-to-one calls. Incoming and outgoing tones continue until the call is accepted, rejected, cancelled, or times out after 30 seconds.

Servers, channels, and events

Communities are organized into servers. Text and voice channels update in real time; members join through invitations and can view scheduled events.

  • Server creation, editing, ownership, and invitation management
  • Text and voice channels with ordering and access permissions
  • Event creation, attendance, and upcoming-event displays
  • Member lists, presence status, and voice-channel participants

Roles, permissions, and moderation

Server owners manage role hierarchy, colors, and capabilities at both server and channel level.

  • Role creation, assignment, ordering, and custom permissions
  • Member removal, bans, timeouts, and mute controls
  • Voice-channel move, mute, deafen, and disconnect controls
  • AutoMod, audit logs, and tracked management actions

Status and Rich Presence

The independent Rich Presence system can display approved games, apps, and media sessions from the desktop application. Detailed privacy controls cover Spotify, YouTube Music, other categories, and visibility levels.

Free customization

Users can choose app and profile themes, accent colors, name fonts and effects, avatar decorations, and profile backgrounds. GIF avatars, profile banners, and server-specific profiles are also free.

  • Ten app themes and eight profile themes
  • Six name fonts plus gradients, glow, and shimmer effects
  • Color-ring, glow, neon, and animated-orbit avatar decorations
  • Custom emoji and stickers with server-specific profile details
  • Messages up to 4,000 characters and 4K/30 screen sharing on supported devices

Windows desktop and web

tahosapp runs in a browser or as a Windows 10/11 application. The desktop app supports startup launch, the system tray, device detection, desktop activity, and automatic updates.

Some features depend on browser permissions, Windows version, hardware, or the role granted by a community administrator.
