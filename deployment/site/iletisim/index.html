What should a support request include?

  • Your username and affected platform (web or Windows)
  • App version and the approximate date and time of the issue
  • The actions you took and the exact error shown
  • A screenshot or relevant log that contains no secrets
Never share: Do not include your password, email verification code, session token, encryption key, or full identity document in a support request.
