Safer communities

Community Guidelines

These core rules help build communities where people can talk, share, and feel safe.

Effective: August 28, 2026

1. No harassment, threats, or hate

Persistent targeted harassment, humiliation, threats, blackmail, stalking, or intimidation are prohibited. Do not promote hatred or violence based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability, or another protected characteristic.

2. Child safety

Child sexual abuse, exploitation, endangerment, and related content are strictly prohibited. Such content will be removed, accounts may be closed, and legally required reports may be made.

3. Respect privacy

Do not publish addresses, phone numbers, identity documents, account information, private images, or conversations without permission. Obtain explicit consent before recording another person's voice, video, or screen.

4. No illegal or dangerous content

Do not use tahosapp for instructions to commit crimes, serious threats of physical harm, terrorist propaganda, drug or weapons trafficking, unauthorized access, or other unlawful activity.

5. No fraud or impersonation

Phishing, fake gifts or prizes, financial fraud, deceptive impersonation, and use of compromised accounts are prohibited.

6. No spam or unwanted contact

Do not send bulk unsolicited messages, invitation spam, repeated advertising, automated friend requests, or bot activity that disrupts communities.

7. Intellectual property

Do not publish copyrighted content, software, or trade secrets that you do not have the right to share. Rights holders may contact support to report infringement.

8. Moderation and enforcement

Depending on severity, harm, repetition, and context, enforcement may include warnings, content removal, timeouts, feature limits, server removal, or account closure. Immediate action may be taken without notice when there is an urgent safety risk.

9. Reporting

Use in-app reporting tools or the support channels. Include the message link, relevant user or server details, and a short explanation while avoiding unnecessary sensitive data.
