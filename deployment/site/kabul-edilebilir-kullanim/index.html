Technical usage rules

Acceptable Use Policy

This policy explains prohibited technical behavior to protect the service, its users, and its infrastructure.

Unauthorized access and security violations

Do not attempt to access another account, server, API, file, or administration area without permission, or bypass authentication, role controls, or security boundaries.

Service disruption

DDoS or DoS activity, connection or memory exhaustion, oversized requests, replay attacks, rate-limit evasion, request flooding, and tests that may disrupt normal service are prohibited.

Bots, automation, and data collection

Without written permission, you may not create accounts or send messages in bulk, collect user data, scrape or index private content, send automated invitations, or operate spam bots. Standard search-engine crawling of public marketing pages is permitted.

Harmful content

Do not distribute malware, remote-access tools, phishing content, session stealers, exploit kits, or files and links intended to harm user devices.

Voice, video, and screen recording

Do not record or republish calls, camera video, or screen shares without the explicit consent of all participants. Stricter recording and notice laws in your location also apply.

Client and protocol

Subject to legal exceptions for interoperability and security research, do not modify the client, update mechanism, or communication protocol to bypass security controls, cheat, or collect user data.

Good-faith security research

Testing must use only your own account and data and must not harm the service. High-volume automated scanning, social engineering, physical attacks, accessing real user data, and establishing persistence are out of scope. Report findings through the private reporting channel.

Enforcement

Traffic that violates this policy may be blocked, and feature, account, or server access may be limited. Serious or legally required matters may be reported to the appropriate authorities.
