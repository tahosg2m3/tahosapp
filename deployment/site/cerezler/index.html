Cookie and Local Storage Policy

This policy explains how tahosapp uses session, security, and preference information on your device.

Effective: August 28, 2026Version: 1.0

What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a browser for a domain. Local storage keeps app preferences and session information on the same device. The desktop application may keep similar information in its secure application storage.

Analytics and advertising

As of this policy's effective date, tahosapp does not use marketing cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics tools that send user behavior to third-party advertising networks. If this changes, the policy will be updated and prior choice will be requested for optional cookies where required by law.

How can you manage storage?

You can remove cookies and site data for tahosapp.com.tr through your browser settings. Signing out or clearing app data in the desktop application may also remove local records. Blocking essential session or security storage may prevent login and real-time features from working.

Third parties

GIPHY may process technical records when GIF search is used, Cloudflare when providing network protection, the email provider during verification, and Spotify when a Spotify link is shared or opened. See the Privacy Policy for details.
